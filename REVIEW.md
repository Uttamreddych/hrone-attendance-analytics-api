# REVIEW.md

List every defect you found in the starter `app/main.py` (helpers and endpoints). For each one:

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `round_half_up` | Used Python's built-in `round()`, which applies banker's rounding (round half to even, e.g. 2.5 rounds to 2). | Test input with .005 or 2.5; output rounds towards even instead of up. | Replaced with `Decimal.quantize(..., rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP)` for standard half-up rounding per Rules R4 and R8. |
| 2 | `truncate_to_seconds` / timestamp conversion | Instants were not truncated to whole seconds before calculations, retaining microsecond noise. | Timestamp `09:40:00.900` treated as after 09:40:00 rather than truncated to `09:40:00`. | Implemented `dt.replace(microsecond=0)` and integer epoch ms division `ms // 1000`. |
| 3 | `is_overnight_shift` & `get_attendance_date` | Overnight shifts (`shift_end <= shift_start`, e.g. 22:00-06:00) were assigned to the punch-in calendar day rather than previous day. | Punch-in at 02:00 IST for a 22:00-06:00 shift got current calendar date instead of previous day. | Added check for `punch_in_ist.time() < shift_end_time` to deduct 1 day for overnight shifts (Rule R1). |
| 4 | `compute_late_minutes` | Counted minutes since grace period end instead of shift start, and missed strict 10-minute threshold (`> 600s`). | 09:40:01 for a 09:30 shift returned 0 or 1 minute instead of 10 minutes. | Applied strict `elapsed_seconds > 600` check and computed floor minutes from `shift_start` (Rule R2). |
| 5 | `compute_work_hours` | Did not truncate to whole seconds before calculation and used float rounding. | Decimal precision drift on fractional hours like 8.425 hours. | Used whole-second truncated instants and `Decimal` division `/ Decimal('3600')` rounded half-up to 2 decimals (Rule R4). |
| 6 | `compute_overtime` | Overtime under 30 minutes was counted; failed across midnight on overnight shifts. | Punch-out 10 minutes after shift end returned 10 min overtime instead of 0; overnight shift end comparison failed. | Enforced `>= 30` minute threshold (otherwise 0) and added day offset to `shift_end` for overnight shifts (Rule R3). |
| 7 | `compute_half_day` | Evaluated unrounded work hours (`work_hours < 4.50`) instead of rounded work hours. | Work hours of 4.496 (rounds to 4.50) was wrongly marked as half day. | Compared `rounded_work_hours < 4.50` after R4 rounding (Rule R5). |
| 8 | `POST /employees` | Missing unique index and race protection; leaked internal MongoDB `_id`; missing email and shift time validation. | Concurrent identical POST requests created duplicate employee records; `_id` field visible in JSON response. | Added idempotent unique index on `emp_code`, caught `DuplicateKeyError` to return 409, stripped `_id`, and added Pydantic validators. |
| 9 | `GET /employees` | Pagination offset calculation error (`skip = page * page_size`); `total` ignored department filter; exposed `_id`. | Page 1 skipped first page of results; `total` counted all documents in collection. | Fixed `skip = (page - 1) * page_size`, applied query filter to `count_documents(q)`, and sorted by `emp_code` ascending. |
| 10 | `POST /attendance/punch-in` | Check-then-insert race condition allowed duplicate records for same employee and date; missing IST timestamp truncation. | Two concurrent punch-ins for same employee created two attendance documents. | Created unique compound index on `("emp_code", "date")`, caught `DuplicateKeyError` to return 409, and truncated timestamps to seconds. |
| 11 | `GET /attendance` | Inverted date range (`date_from > date_to`) was not validated (returned empty list); sort order did not match spec. | Passing `date_from=2026-07-10&date_to=2026-07-05` returned 200 instead of 422. | Added 422 validation for inverted date range and sorted cursor by `date` descending then `emp_code` ascending. |
| 12 | `POST /attendance/punch-out` | Not concurrency-safe; allowed punch-outs without punch-in, punch-outs before punch-in, or > 24 hours later. | Two concurrent punch-out requests both succeeded; negative work hours allowed. | Used atomic `find_one_and_update({"punch_out": None})` for 409 on conflict, validated timestamp ordering (422), and recomputed R3-R5. |
| 13 | `PATCH /attendance/{emp_code}/{date}` | Concurrent updates silently lost audit history; allowed punch times with ABSENT/LEAVE; allowed no-op edits. | Concurrent regularizations overwrote each other's history entries; changing nothing returned 200 instead of 422. | Used optimistic locking on `history` array, validated status-punch constraints (422), rejected no-op requests (422), and tracked only changed fields. |
| 14 | Database Indexes | No indexes were created at startup, causing full collection scans on queries. | Aggregation and listing queries performed `COLLSCAN` on large datasets. | Implemented `ensure_indexes()` creating indexes for unique keys, composite filters, and sorting at startup. |

### Examined and Decided Was Not a Defect
- **`GET /health` returning 503:** We verified that `/health` returning 503 on database ping failure is the intended Kubernetes readiness probe behavior defined in `openapi.yaml`.
- **Calendar Dates as Strings:** Storing `date`, `joined_on`, and `shift_start`/`shift_end` as strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `HH:MM`) was examined and confirmed to be intentional by `DATA_MODEL.md` to avoid timezone corruption.
